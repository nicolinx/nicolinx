### [Character Lens](https://github.com/nicolinx/character-lens)

Draw a Chinese or Japanese character with your finger and look it up. Handwriting is recognized on-device with ML Kit, readings come from an offline dictionary of 60,183 characters, and Gemini writes a short explanation grounded in the dictionary entry. A validator flags example readings the dictionary can't confirm. 630 tests, ADRs, and a [case study](https://github.com/nicolinx/character-lens/blob/main/docs/case-study.md) on how it was built.
